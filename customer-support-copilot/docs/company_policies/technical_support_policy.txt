Technical Support Policy

Technical support handles product issues such as:
- Battery draining quickly.
- Device not turning on.
- Software errors.
- Connectivity problems.
- App crashes.
- Performance issues.
- Installation failures.
- Error messages.
- Slow system behavior.

Support agents should collect:
- Product name.
- Issue description.
- Error messages.
- Device model.
- Troubleshooting steps already tried.

Critical technical issues should be escalated to the Engineering or Specialist Support queue.

Technical issues affecting product usability may be marked as medium or high priority depending on severity.
