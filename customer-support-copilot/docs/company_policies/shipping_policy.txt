Shipping Policy

Standard delivery usually takes 5 to 7 business days.

Customers can contact support when:
- The order is delayed.
- Tracking information is not updating.
- The package is marked as delivered but not received.
- The wrong delivery address was used.
- The package is lost during transit.

For delayed shipments, support agents should first check the tracking status.

For lost packages, the support team should verify courier information before offering replacement or refund.

Shipping issues should usually be assigned to the Logistics or Customer Support queue.
Urgent delivery failures for premium customers may be marked as high priority.
