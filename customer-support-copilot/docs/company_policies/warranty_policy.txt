Warranty Policy

Products include a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.

Warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects.
- Hardware failure under normal use.
- Battery or charging defects.
- Product malfunction not caused by misuse.
- Device failure within the warranty period.

Warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage caused by the customer.
- Water damage.
- Unauthorized repair.
- Accidental damage.
- Damage after warranty expiry.

Eligible customers may receive repair, replacement, or service support.

Warranty-related tickets should be assigned to Technical Support or Warranty Support.
